cacio咖啡

Italian term that generally refers to cheese, commonly used in traditional dishes from central Italy, such as Lazio. It is most famously associated with the dish 'Cacio e Pepe,' which features Pecorino Romano cheese and black pepper.意大利语，通常指奶酪，通常用于意大利中部的传统菜肴，如拉齐奥。它最着名的是与“Cacio e Pepe”一道菜有关，其中包括Pecorino Romano奶酪和黑胡椒。
