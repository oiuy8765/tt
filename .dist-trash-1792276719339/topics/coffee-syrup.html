Coffee syrup咖啡糖浆

Sweet, thick syrup made by straining hot water and sugar through coffee grounds. It is commonly used to make coffee milk, a popular drink in Rhode Island, USA, by mixing the syrup with milk, providing a unique coffee-flavored beverage.咖啡糖浆：一种甜而稠的糖浆，由热水和糖通过咖啡渣过滤而成。通常用于制作咖啡牛奶，这是美国罗得岛的一种流行饮料，将糖浆与牛奶混合，提供一种独特的咖啡风味饮料
