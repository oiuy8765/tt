shoyuShoyu

Japanese soy sauce made from soybeans, wheat, salt, and a fermenting agent. It comes in two main types: koikuchi (dark soy sauce) and usukuchi (light soy sauce), and is used in various dishes such as sushi, sashimi, soups, and marinades.日本酱料由大豆、小麦、盐和一种发酵剂制成。主要有两种类型：koikuchi（深色酱料）和usukuchi（浅色酱料），用于寿司、生鱼片、汤和腌泡汁等各种菜肴。
