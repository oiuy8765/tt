Anti colic baby bottle防绞痛奶瓶

A baby bottle designed to minimize the amount of air ingested by infants during feeding, thereby reducing gas bubbles in the stomach. This type of bottle helps to slow down the intake of food, which can alleviate colic symptoms in babies.一种婴儿奶瓶，旨在减少婴儿在喂奶时摄入的空气量，从而减少胃中的气泡。这种奶瓶有助于减缓食物的摄入，从而可以减轻婴儿的绞痛症状。
