Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information TrendSift (the domain name trendsift.site) processes, why it does so, and how you can control it.Our basic principles are simple:Don't need an account number, don't sell data, collect less if you can..

Effective Date: 17 October 2026 Version: v1.0 Applicable site: trendsift.site

1. one-sentence summary

This site is a pure browsing data tool station. YouNo registration required, no login required, no personal information requiredWe don't create profiles, we don't sell, we don't rent anything, and the only messages that leave your browser are the ones you write down when you send us an email.

Your favorite keywords, Chinese translation switch, the last choice of carrier, are stored in your browser local (localStorage), It won't be uploaded to our servers.. Clear browser data to delete completely.

2. What information we process

2.1 Information you volunteered.

There's only one way: you passContactThe email address on this page will be sent to us. We will therefore receive your email address, the body of your email and whatever you choose to attach to it. This information will only be used to respond to your questions, process data correction or business communications, and will not be used for marketing and will not be added to any external lists.

2.2 Automatically generated technical logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare's static hosting service. In order to serve the page, prevent abuse and troubleshoot, the hosting party records access logs according to industry practice, usually including IP address, browser type and version, operating system, access time, requested page address, source page (Referer) and HTTP status code.

Our servers have no database, no membership system, and we do not associate these logs with any personal identity for analysis. The logs are stored by hosting providers according to their own retention policies, and we only view them on demand to troubleshoot or respond to attacks.

2.3 Save information locally in your browser

In order to keep the same usage status the next time you open it, this site will store the following items locally in your browser:

  • gt_saved: Keywords you have collected in My List (keyword ID only, no personal information).
  • gt_zh: Chinese translation switch status.
  • gt_carrier: The carrier you last selected (all/websites/physical products).

This data exists only on your device, and we can say "invisible" rather than "invisible"-it's never sent to the server at all. You can delete site data in your browser settings, or click "Empty List" in "My List" and delete it at any time.
