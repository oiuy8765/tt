Hojicha latteHojicha拿铁

A tea latte made from Japanese roasted green tea (hojicha), water, and milk. It is typically prepared using Hojicha Powder, which is produced from finely milled roasted tea leaves, allowing for the consumption of the entire tea leaves.由日本烤绿色茶（Hojicha），水和牛奶制成的茶拿铁。通常使用Hojicha粉末制备，该粉末由精细研磨的烤茶叶制成，允许整个茶叶食用。
